# Lucas Arango’s portfolio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors exploring Lucas’s software engineering work, public projects, and published writing.

## Product Purpose

Present Lucas’s factual biography and make his projects, source code, writing, and résumé easy to explore.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve published URLs and frontmatter, factual content, SEO, keyboard access, mobile behavior, reduced motion, the forest game’s scoring, and lazy loading of the game and Ask transcript. No new infrastructure, paid services, external AI calls during testing, or production messages.

## Brand Commitments

Personal character and readable résumé/writing take priority over studio branding. Lead with who Lucas is and print his name once per page. The visual direction and rejected alternatives are recorded in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## Evidence on Hand

Public `src/components/intro.tsx`, `src/lib/projects.ts`, `src/lib/site.ts`, `_posts/`, and the existing public résumé feed. These are the source of truth. No invented accomplishments, endorsements, private personal facts, or metrics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Lightweight mobile-first behavior, semantic navigation, visible focus, touch-sized controls, readable writing, and reduced-motion alternatives.

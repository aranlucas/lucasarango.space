# Portfolio redesign

Mode: Experience on home; Read for writing and résumé. Scope: public frontend presentation. Selection and implementation delegated by the user; code-first for this session to make the actual interaction the visual source. No stored workflow default.

## Explored directions

Three substantially different directions: an editorial field notebook (literary, quiet, familiar), a typographic studio index (poster-scale project titles and a dense archive), and a systems atlas (relational diagrams, saturated cobalt, navigable routes through actual work). Chosen: systems atlas.

Grounded candidate list: field notebook; conference proceedings; independent software studio index; hiking trail guide; research exhibit wall; systems atlas inspired by transit planning diagrams; engineering monograph. Impeccable seed 256abd80 assigned candidate 6.

Challenger judgments on audience identification / product clarity: split-flap concourse competitive on identification, declined as a build because invented live states obscure permanent writing; HyperCard competitive on play, lower clarity for long reading; tensegrity declined on both (force semantics do not describe the work); provenance ribbon competitive on clarity, lower identification (custody differs from building); noir poster declined on both; fashion mook declined on both (no factual garment/photographic carrier). The compact alternate paths remain notebook and studio index as asked in the brief.

Raises: concourse donates strong stable alignment; HyperCard donates reversible exploration; tensegrity donates visible relationships; provenance donates attached source evidence; noir donates total typographic commitment; mook donates varied exhibit scale. These are disciplines, not borrowed costumes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: useful tools are connected to the real problems that inspired them. A navigable systems atlas replaces the small biography column and repeated list/card template.

OWN-WORLD: decisive cobalt, warm white, ink, and acid lime; industrial condensed display, legible sans body, square surfaces, thick route lines and open exhibit layouts.

STORY: meet Lucas, trace a personal idea into a useful tool, explore three verified projects, then read the published decisions or résumé.

FIRST VIEWPORT: oversized Lucas Arango at left with Seattle/software engineer identity and concise original introduction; interactive cobalt project route at right, a lime line connecting three semantic selectable stages. Direct project and writing actions sit below the introduction. The complete biography follows with original facts unchanged.

FORM: systems atlas, grounded candidate 6, seed 256abd80. Signature interaction: Follow an idea, a reversible route explorer over factual project workflows. Touch and keyboard operate native buttons; reduced motion changes state immediately.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
